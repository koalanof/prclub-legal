Common questions

How does verification work?

Submit a race or exercise with evidence (a bib photo, medal photo, or results link) and a human reviewer checks it against what you logged. Approved results get a checkmark on your profile; declined ones keep the entry, just without the checkmark.

How many verification reviews do I get?

Free accounts get 1 review, total. PRCLUB Pro members get 3 reviews every month, whether each one is approved or declined.

What's included in PRCLUB Pro?

Unlimited races, exercises, and upcoming events, plus 3 verification reviews a month.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In the app: Settings > Get Support > Manage Subscription. That opens Apple's own subscription settings, since PRCLUB never handles billing directly.

Can other people see my races?

Only what's tied to your public username: your races, exercises, and Grind check-ins, plus whether each is verified.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings > Account > Delete Account & All Data. This is permanent — it removes your public profile and every race, exercise, and Grind check-in, both from your device and from PRCLUB entirely.
