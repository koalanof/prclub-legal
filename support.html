Common questions

How does verification work?

Submit a race or exercise with evidence (a bib photo, medal photo, results link, or a video) and a certified coach checks it against what you logged. Most decisions come back within 24 hours. Approved results keep a checkmark for good; declined ones keep the entry, just without the checkmark, plus a note saying what was missing.

What does verification cost?

Every account gets 1 verification free. After that they are $4.99 for one or $12.99 for three. These are one-time purchases, not a subscription, nothing renews, and they never expire. Ten weeks of a Grind streak earns another free one.

What happens if my submission is declined?

You keep the entry without the checkmark, and the coach leaves a note explaining what was missing. The verification is used up either way, since the review still took a person's time, so check your evidence is readable before you send it. Fix what was missing and you can resubmit with another verification. If you think a decision was wrong, email us and we will sort it out.

Why do I need a complete profile before submitting?

A verified result sits on your Athlete CV, so it needs a photo, name, bio and location to sit on. Fill those in once and you will not be asked again.

I have the old PRCLUB Pro subscription. What now?

Pro is not sold any more, but if you are still subscribed it keeps working exactly as before, 3 verifications every 30 days, until you cancel. To cancel: Settings > Get Support > Manage Subscription, which opens Apple's own settings, since PRCLUB never handles billing directly.

Can other people see my races?

Only what's tied to your public username: your races, exercises, and Grind check-ins, plus whether each is verified.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings > Account > Delete Account & All Data. This is permanent — it removes your public profile and every race, exercise, and Grind check-in, both from your device and from PRCLUB entirely.
