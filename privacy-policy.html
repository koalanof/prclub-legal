Privacy Policy

PRCLUB: Verified PR Tracker · Last updated August 15, 2026

PRCLUB ("we," "us") is a mobile app for logging races and exercises, having them verified, and sharing a public athletic profile. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • Your name — from Sign in with Apple, only if you choose to share it.
  • A stable account identifier — provided by Sign in with Apple; this is how the app recognizes your account. We never see your Apple ID email or password.
  • Username, bio, and location — whatever you enter in your profile.
  • Photos and videos — evidence you attach to a race or exercise, your profile photo, and your daily Grind check-in photos.
  • Race and exercise details — event names, dates, times, results, and notes you enter yourself.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or any data for tracking you across other companies' apps or websites. PRCLUB shows no ads.

How it's used

Everything above exists to run the app's core features: syncing your data across your own devices, showing your public profile and verified results to other athletes (only for whatever you've set to public), and letting a human reviewer confirm evidence you submit for verification.

Where it's stored

All data is stored in Apple's CloudKit, under our app's iCloud container. Your races, exercises, and settings sync privately to your own iCloud account. Anything you publish under your username (races, exercises, Grind check-ins, verification submissions) is stored in a public CloudKit database so other athletes' devices can display it — this is the only way that data becomes visible to anyone but you.

We don't run our own servers and don't share your data with any third party other than Apple, who provides the CloudKit infrastructure and processes subscription payments through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Your choices

  • Camera and Photo Library access are requested only when you attach evidence or take a Grind check-in photo, and only used for that purpose.
  • Notifications (a daily Grind reminder, and an alert when a verification submission is reviewed) can be turned off any time in Settings.
  • Deleting your account: Settings > Account > Delete Account & All Data permanently removes your public profile, every race/exercise/Grind check-in, and all data tied to your account, both from your device and from PRCLUB's servers. This can't be undone.

Children

PRCLUB is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
